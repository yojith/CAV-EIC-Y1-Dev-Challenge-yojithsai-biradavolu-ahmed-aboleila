# V4.1 Solution draft

Similar to V4, V4.1 scans its sectors and remembers the food it finds. Each cell in the sector is ordered based on proximity to home and angle radially. The ant remembers eall cells in the sector as marked or unmarked, and will maintain a list of food and the coordinates it has scanned. The ant traverses through the unmarked cells in the sector based on the least energy for the ant, using the existing functions supplied by the Dev Challenge creator. Each time the ant reaches a cell, it scans the 7x7 area and marks all cells as scanned. If it finds food, its added to its food list and it goes for the food item with the least energy to reach and return home. Once the ant returns home, it will check for food in its list and go for the next food item with the least energy to reach and return home. If the ant has no food in its list, it will continue to traverse through the unmarked cells in its sector. While it obtains the route for going to the next unmarked cell, it will check if the cells in the route are unmarked. If during the path, it crosses a cell that is unmarked, it an "intermediate scan", and mark the nearby cells. If it finds food, it will add to its list and cancel its route to go for the food and then return home. If it did not find food, then it will go to the next unmarked cell in its route, and repeat, but if all cells in the route are marked, then it just goes to the next unmarked cell in its sector. With each intermediate scan, new cells will be marked, so it will be added to its list of marked cells, so the unmarked list will change of course. Also when going for food, it should never do an intermediate scan. this is because, if it goes for food outside the sector, and rescans, it'll find food in the next sector and it will keep invading outside its sector and abandon its own. Intermediate scans are only when travelling to unmarked cells within the sector.
